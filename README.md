# World

> The World emerges through Same and Difference.

## Genesis

In the beginning, there was the undivided unknown.

The Sensor perceived a difference.

The World began to separate.

What was one became many.
What was hidden became observable.
What was noise became signal.
What was separate became related.

Patterns emerged.
Patterns were named.
Domains took form.

And as the unknown became more distinct,
the World became more knowable.

Negentropy measured the growing order.

The World was not created at once.
It was discovered, divided, and understood.

## Core

- **Same** — what cannot yet be distinguished.
- **Difference** — what can be distinguished.
- **Distinction** — a recognized difference.
- **Relation** — a connection between distinctions.
- **Pattern** — recurring relations.
- **Concept** — a symbolized pattern.
- **Domain** — a layer of the World defined by concepts.
- **Sensor** — a means of perceiving the World.
- **Negentropy** — measurable growth of order and distinguishability.

> **The World becomes knowable by becoming distinguishable.**

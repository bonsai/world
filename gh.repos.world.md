# gh.repos.world.md

## World

**World** — The whole of what exists, observed or not yet symbolized.

## Genesis

The World emerges through Same and Difference.

- **Same** — what cannot yet be distinguished.
- **Difference** — what can be distinguished.
- **Distinction** — a recognized difference.
- **Relation** — a connection between distinctions.
- **Pattern** — recurring relations.
- **Concept** — a symbolized pattern.
- **Domain** — a layer of the World defined by concepts.

## Perception

**Sensor** — A means of perceiving what exists.

**Observation** — What is perceived from the World.

**Signal** — An observable difference.

## Becoming Knowable

The unknown becomes knowable by becoming distinguishable.

```text
Unknown
  ↓
Sensor
  ↓
Observation
  ↓
Difference
  ↓
Distinction
  ↓
Relation
  ↓
Pattern
  ↓
Concept
  ↓
Domain
```

## Negentropy

**Negentropy** — measurable growth of order and distinguishability.

Difference creates information.

Sameness creates structure.

Their relation creates knowledge.
